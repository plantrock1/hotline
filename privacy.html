Privacy Policy

Hotline brand, operated by Jack Zuckerman (sole proprietor). Last updated November 23, 2026.

This policy explains what Hotline brand collects when you call the artist hotline at +1 (740) 596-6665 or the UK hotline, and how that information is used.

What we collect

  • The phone number you call from, which phone networks share automatically with every call.
  • The approximate location your phone carrier attaches to that number (country, state or region, city).
  • The date and time of your call.
  • Any text replies you send to the hotline number, such as STOP or HELP.

How we use it

  • To send you one text message after your call with a link to the artist's music or news.
  • To keep a list of callers so we know how many people used the hotline.
  • To honor opt-out requests. If you reply STOP, we will not text you again.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your number is processed only by the service providers that run the hotline (our telephone and messaging provider, Twilio, and our spreadsheet provider, Google), and only to deliver the service described here.

How long we keep it

We keep caller records for as long as the hotline campaign runs, and delete them within 12 months after it ends. You can ask us to delete your number at any time.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Email jack.zuckerman@hallwood.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.
